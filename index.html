About us

We study what happens when gravitation and quantum theory are taken seriously at the same time. Our work sits at the interface between general relativity, quantum field theory in curved spacetimes, and quantum information theory — a place where questions about entropy, measurement, and the structure of spacetime merge all together.

Concretely, we work on relativistic quantum information and communication in curved spacetimes, on the infrared structure of gravity and asymptotic symmetries, on black hole thermodynamics and entropy bounds, and on bottom-up approaches to low-energy quantum gravity.

The group is based at the Federal University of ABC in Santo André, Brazil, and is led by André G. S. Landulfo. Our research is supported by FAPESP, CAPES, and CNPq.

We run InfoGraviton, a weekly journal club on information, quantum theory, and gravity. Its full archive of talks and reading suggestions is on this site, and visitors are welcome.
